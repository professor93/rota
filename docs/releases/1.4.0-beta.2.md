## rota 1.4.0-beta.2

### Changed for people coming from 1.4.0-beta.1

- **An organisation's cached policy and remote settings are each account's own.** `policy-limits.json`, `remote-settings.json` and every name that is one of them followed by a dot, such as `policy-limits.json.signature.json` or `remote-settings.json.synced-at`, are no longer linked from your Claude directory into an account's home, so one organisation's policy or settings never apply under another account. A link an older rota made for one of them goes on the first launch that finds the home quiet; a real file of that name in the home, and your own files, are left as they are.
